INTERPRETATION GUIDE
======================================================================

Radial Density Profile:
  - Shows how atoms are distributed at different distances from droplet center
  - Peak locations indicate preferred atomic shells
  - Higher density = more atoms at that distance

Normalized g(r):
  - Values > 1: Higher concentration than bulk average
  - Values < 1: Lower concentration (depletion)
  - Values ≈ 1: Uniform distribution (bulk-like)

Cumulative Distribution:
  - Shows total number of atoms within a given radius
  - Useful for determining droplet size and structure
